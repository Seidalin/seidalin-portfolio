## Background

HCB Business launched as an MVP in 6 months (2023) — two currency accounts, basic payments. Then 15+ products got layered on top of the MVP architecture. The app had outgrown its foundation — functional, but cluttered and inconsistent.

**When presenting our annual design strategy, I made the redesign a priority.** The architecture couldn't scale, clients struggled with navigation, and retail and business apps needed a unified platform. I built ~80% of the App Vision deck that secured C-level buy-in. Design-led, not business-requested.

## Pre-Authorization Zone

**Before:** Static splash, two buttons. No reason to explore before committing.

**After:** Product info, tariffs, offers. Quick access to branches, FAQ, rates. Registration split by entity type with guidance.

**Result:** 55% → 76% conversion (+38%).

---

## Home Screen

**Before:** Dense, overloaded. Users couldn't find bank details or statements.

**After:** Quick actions, unified tab bar with retail, "My Bank" section, 3 accounts max, full customization.

**Result:** Stickiness +23%. Sessions/user +15%.

---

## Merchant Sales Cabinet

Core audience — merchants. Sales Cabinet was buried in navigation. Promoted to **5th tab**. One tap.

**Result:** MAU +19%. DAU +13%. New merchants +40%. Revenue +21%.

## Process & Team

4 designers × 2-3 streams. Redesign on top of regular workload — no dedicated squad. Weekly critiques, specs-and-tokens handoff, post-release VoC tracking. No decision shipped without data.

---

## Design System

Token-based architecture (Figma ↔ code via Token Studio). Unified design language — business + retail apps share visuals for the first time. Light/dark mode. 95% design-to-dev consistency (QA audits).
